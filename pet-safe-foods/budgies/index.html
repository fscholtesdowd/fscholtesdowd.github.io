What can budgies eat?

Budgies can eat: Apple; Carrot; Broccoli; Blueberry; Grapes; Sweet potato. Feed only a little: Seed mixes & millet; Iceberg lettuce & celery; Dairy (cheese, yogurt, milk). Never feed: Avocado; Chocolate, caffeine & alcohol; Very salty foods (chips, pretzels, popcorn); Processed, fried & buttery foods.

Every line below is traced to a named source (3 veterinary or animal-welfare sources, listed at the bottom). If a food is not on this page, no source we use covers it for budgies, so we do not list it either way. This is a feeding guide, not vet care: if a budgie looks sick or stops eating, call a vet.

What the vets say

  • Pellets should represent approximately 60-80% of your bird's diet. (VCA Animal Hospitals)
  • Fruits, vegetables, and greens should account for 20-25% of the daily diet at most. (VCA Animal Hospitals)
  • Small parrot species - such as budgerigars, parrotlets, lovebirds and cockatiels - do need some seeds each day (though these should make up no more than a tenth of their diet) (RSPCA)
  • As a rule, any wholesome, nutritious foods that you and your family eat, your bird can eat, but in very small quantities (a thumbnail size amount for a budgie is equivalent to a dinner plate size portion for a person and is appropriate). (VCA Animal Hospitals)
  • Budgerigars fed 1 g of avocado fruit developed agitation and feather pulling, and ingestion of 8.7 g of mashed avocado fruit resulted in death within 48 hours. (Merck Veterinary Manual)
