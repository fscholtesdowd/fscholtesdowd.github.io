What can ferrets eat?

Ferrets can eat: Ferret-specific dry kibble; Whole-meat diets (properly balanced); Growth-formula food (for kits); Pregnancy/nursing-formula food; Senior-formula food; Fresh water. Feed only a little: Cooked meat or meat baby food; "Duck soup" liquid supplement; Any other people food or snack food. Never feed: Chocolate; Sweets & sugary treats; Dairy products; Raisins; Fresh fruit; Vegetables.

Every line below is traced to a named source (1 veterinary or animal-welfare sources, listed at the bottom). If a food is not on this page, no source we use covers it for ferrets, so we do not list it either way. This is a feeding guide, not vet care: if a ferret looks sick or stops eating, call a vet.

What the vets say

  • A good quality ferret diet should contain 32% to 40% protein and 10% to 15% fat. (VCA Animal Hospitals)
  • A good quality ferret diet should contain 32% to 40% protein and 10% to 15% fat. (VCA Animal Hospitals)
  • Ferrets are carnivores and cannot digest a diet that contains more than 4% fiber. (VCA Animal Hospitals)
  • Ferrets have a rapid gut transit time (from eating to defecating) of three to four hours, so they appear to eat and defecate constantly. (VCA Animal Hospitals)
