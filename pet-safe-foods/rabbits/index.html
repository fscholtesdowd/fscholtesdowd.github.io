What can rabbits eat?

Rabbits can eat: Grass hay - timothy and other grass hay; Leaf lettuces - romaine, red leaf, green leaf; Parsley; Cilantro; Dandelion greens & leaves; Basil; Celery; Broccoli; Greens to rotate - kale, watercress, mint, bok choy, beet tops; Plain grass-hay pellets - a measured scoop. Feed only a little: Carrots - the orange root; Fresh fruit - apple, berries, banana; Spinach; Alfalfa hay & alfalfa-based pellets; Any NEW food. Never feed: Avocado - fruit, seeds, stems & leaves; Onion & garlic; Rhubarb; Chocolate; Bread, pasta, crackers & breakfast cereal; Seeds, nuts, dried corn & muesli-style mixes; Potatoes - raw, cooked, or unripe; Corn, beans & peas; Fresh-cut lawn clippings.

Every line below is traced to a named source (5 veterinary or animal-welfare sources, listed at the bottom). If a food is not on this page, no source we use covers it for rabbits, so we do not list it either way. This is a feeding guide, not vet care: if a rabbit looks sick or stops eating, call a vet.

What the vets say

  • 85% of a rabbit's diet should consist of hay and fresh grass. (RSPCA (UK))
  • 1-2 cups of vegetables for every four (4) lbs. of body weight. ... at least three types of green leafy vegetables daily. (House Rabbit Society)
  • 18% minimum fiber ... a small part of your rabbit's diet. (House Rabbit Society)
  • A very small amount, once or twice a week - fruit and carrots are treats, not food. (House Rabbit Society)
  • Fresh, clean water at all times. Change it twice a day and check bottles for blockages, algae, or freezing. (RSPCA (UK))
