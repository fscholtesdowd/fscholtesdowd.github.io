What can bearded dragons eat?

Bearded Dragons can eat: Collard, turnip & dandelion greens; Bell peppers & bok choy; Broccoli & cilantro; Calcium-dusted crickets, mealworms & dubia roaches; Silkworms & hornworms; Fresh water. Feed only a little: Fruit (apple, banana, grapes/raisins, mango); Flowers (hibiscus, dandelions, roses); Waxworms & superworms; Pinkie or fuzzy mice; Swiss chard, spinach & beet greens; Cabbage, kale & mustard greens. Never feed: Fireflies; Wild-caught insects (yard or garden); Feeding on loose sand, walnut shell, or calci-sand.

Every line below is traced to a named source (2 veterinary or animal-welfare sources, listed at the bottom). If a food is not on this page, no source we use covers it for bearded dragons, so we do not list it either way. This is a feeding guide, not vet care: if a bearded dragon looks sick or stops eating, call a vet.

What the vets say

  • A bearded dragon's diet should generally be about 50% plant-based and 50% animal-based (insect) material, although it varies depending on their age. (VCA Animal Hospitals)
  • Most of the plant-based material you feed your bearded dragon (80% to 90%) should be leafy green vegetables. (VCA Animal Hospitals)
  • Fruit should comprise only 10% to 20% of the plants fed to bearded dragons (along with yellow, red, and orange vegetables). (VCA Animal Hospitals)
  • you can lightly sprinkle their food with a calcium powder that contains vitamin D3, two to three times per week. (VCA Animal Hospitals)
  • All insects should be gut-loaded (fed nutritious food that is then passed on to the lizard) within 12 hours of being fed to your dragon. (VCA Animal Hospitals)
