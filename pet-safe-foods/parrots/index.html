What can parrots eat?

Parrots can eat: A nutritionally complete parrot pellet; Carrots & broccoli; Peas; De-seeded apple; Grapes; Pomegranate; Blueberries & sprouts. Feed only a little: Seeds & nuts as an occasional treat; Cooked egg, lean meat, fish & a little cheese; Any wholesome family food, tiny amounts; Iceberg lettuce & celery; Extra daily seeds - budgies, lovebirds, cockatiels & parrotlets only. Never feed: Avocado; Onion; Chocolate; Caffeine - coffee, tea & soda; Alcohol.

Every line below is traced to a named source (3 veterinary or animal-welfare sources, listed at the bottom). If a food is not on this page, no source we use covers it for parrots, so we do not list it either way. This is a feeding guide, not vet care: if a parrot looks sick or stops eating, call a vet.

What the vets say

  • Pellets should ideally represent approximately 75%-80% of the bird's diet. (VCA Animal Hospitals)
  • About one quarter should be washed fruits and vegetables, such as: (RSPCA (UK))
  • Budgies, parrotlets, lovebirds and cockatiels need some seeds every day, but even for them, seeds should stay under a tenth of the diet. (RSPCA (UK))
  • An all-seed diet can cause obesity, fatty liver disease, and vitamin A and calcium deficiency. (RSPCA (UK), Merck Vet. Manual)
  • Galahs, macaws, Amazon parrots, and Quaker parrots are named as especially prone to obesity from a high-fat diet. (Merck Vet. Manual)
