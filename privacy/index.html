Privacy Policy

Plain version: these tools don't ask for your name, your email, or an account. We don't watch what you do here.

What we collect

Nothing. These pages carry no analytics script, no tracking pixel, and no cookie of our own. There is no sign-up form and no login anywhere on these sites. GitHub Pages, which hosts these tools for free, keeps its own basic server logs (like any web host) — we don't have access to those and don't request them.

Affiliate links

Some pages carry links to other stores (for example True Leaf Market). If you click through and buy something, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. That's the whole arrangement — we don't see what you bought, your payment details, or your address. The store you land on has its own privacy policy, which governs from the moment you leave our page.

Outside links

These pages also link out to sources we used (NOAA, university extension offices, etc.), our newsletter sign-up, and our shop listings (Etsy, Gumroad). Each of those is a separate site with its own privacy policy — ours only covers the page you're reading.

Questions

These tools are built and run by one person (Forrest Scholtes) as a hobby project, not a company. A dedicated contact address for these tools is being set up — check back here, or reach out through the newsletter sign-up link in any page's footer in the meantime.
